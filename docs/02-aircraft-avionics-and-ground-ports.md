# SN-V1 Aircraft, Avionics & Ground Infrastructure Specification

## 1. Aircraft Platform (`SN-V1 Hybrid VTOL` — Mind Map 2.1)
- **Airframe Configuration:** VTOL Fixed-Wing Hybrid combining efficient wing-borne suburban corridor cruise (`25 m/s`) with centimeter-accurate vertical rooftop and residential landing.
- **Payload Capacity:** `2.0 – 5.0 kg` modular belly bay sized for standard family restaurant meals, pharmacy prescriptions, or suburban grocery bags.
- **Thermal Order Integrity:** Active climate-controlled payload inserts (`65°C` hot meal zone / `2–4°C` chilled grocery & biologic cold-chain zone).
- **Acoustic Propulsion:** Shrouded **Electric Ducted Fans** engineered for blade-passing frequency suppression delivering **`< 55 dB @ 50m`** (quieter than ambient suburban conversation).
- **Propulsion Redundancy:** Hexa/Octo-rotor distributed lift architecture with full single/dual motor-out controlled landing capability + ASTM F3322 autonomous ballistic parachute recovery.

## 2. Autonomy & Avionics (Mind Map 2.2)
- **Detect-and-Avoid (DAA):** Onboard multi-sensor fusion combining **4D mmWave Radar**, **Stereo Optical Computer Vision**, and **ADS-B In** for cooperative and non-cooperative traffic deconfliction.
- **Precision Landing:** Dual-band **RTK-GPS (`<2 cm` CEP)** fused with encrypted downward **Optical AprilTag Fiducials** on merchant rooftop ports and residential drop pads.
- **All-Weather Hardening:** **IP54+** ingress protection with **`15 m/s` (`33 mph`) sustained wind tolerance** and active battery pack thermal conditioning.

1. **Merchant Rooftop Drone Ports:**
   - **90-Second Turnaround:** Robotic battery pack swap + automated belly parcel load/unload.
   - **Charging Network:** Distributed 3-phase fast-charging magazine (`20-minute` pack replenishment).
   - **Zero Real-Estate CapEx:** Hosted on merchant rooftops or 2-spot parking-lot canopies via REIT agreements.
2. **Central Vertiports:**
   - Hub-and-spoke fleet staging, `1:10–1:15` Remote Pilot Operations Center, daily pre-flight swappable-module **Line Maintenance**, and `500-hour / 6-month` **Heavy Maintenance** overhauls.
   - **Dispatch Reliability SLA:** **`> 99.5%`** (`99.7%` pilot corridor telemetry).
3. **Suburban Drop Zones:**
   - Driveway Fiducial Markers, HOA Curbside Pads, Apartment Smart Lockers, and Residential "Drone Mailboxes".
