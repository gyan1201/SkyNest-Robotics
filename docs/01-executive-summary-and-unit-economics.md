# SkyNest Robotics — Executive Summary & Unit Economics

## 1. Mission & Market Thesis
**SkyNest Robotics** builds an open, POS-agnostic **Retail and Restaurant Drone Network** engineered for suburban corridors (`8,000–15,000 pop/sq mi`).

- **Replaces:** Gig delivery drivers (`$8–$15` per delivery, `30–60 min` congested ground ETAs, `20–30%` marketplace take-rates).
- **At-Scale Economics:** `$1–$3` per delivery (`$1.50` loaded variable cost target) at `30+` deliveries per drone-day (`32` peak sorties = `4 flights/hr × 8 peak hrs`).
- **Disruption Magnitude:** **3–5× delivery cost reduction** across a **$150B+ US Food & Parcel Last-Mile TAM** (`$45B` suburban `<5mi` SAM → `$500M` Year 5 SOM).

---

## 2. Revenue Streams (Mind Map 3.1)
1. **Per-Delivery Fee (`$3.00–$5.00`):** Charged flat to the merchant (`$4.00` target average), replacing `$8–$15` gig delivery fees and `20–30%` DoorDash/UberEats commissions (`0%` SkyNest commission).
2. **Fleet Access & Software Subscription:** Monthly SaaS seat & rooftop port telemetry fee (`$299/mo` per store location).
3. **Aggregated Data & Promoted Placement:** Suburban corridor demand heatmaps and sponsored merchant discovery slots.
