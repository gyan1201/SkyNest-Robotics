# FAA Regulatory Pathway, Waivers & Community Charter

## 1. Staged FAA Certification Pathway (Mind Map 5.1)
- **Phase 1 (Year 1 — Active):** Commercial operations under **14 CFR Part 107** + operational waivers (VLOS/EVLOS, Night Operations, Operations Over People) to capture `500+` safety, reliability, and `<55 dB` community acoustic validation sorties.
- **Phase 2 (Year 2–3 — Application Filed):** **14 CFR Part 135 Standard Air Carrier Certificate** (following the FAA precedent established by Wing Aviation and UPS Flight Forward) unlocking scheduled **BVLOS** commercial package delivery across multi-metro suburban corridors.
- **Phase 3 (Year 4+):** **Type Certification (`14 CFR §21.17(b)`) + Production Certificate** enabling scalable manufacturing (`1,000+` custom airframes) and harmonized international expansion (**EASA EU**, **Transport Canada**, **CASA Australia**).

## 3. Community, Privacy & Equity Commitments (Mind Map 5.3)
1. **Noise Mitigation:** Electric ducted fans (`<55 dB @ 50m`) paired with dynamic flight-path altitude and lateral corridor rotation.
2. **Residential Privacy by Hardware Design:** Downward cameras never record private property video; optical vision hardware processes encrypted landing fiducial tags exclusively during final descent.
3. **Food Desert & Low-Income Corridor Equity:** Binding municipal commitment dedicating subsidized `$3.00` flat-fee grocery and prescription delivery corridors to underserved suburban food deserts.
