# Go-to-Market Strategy, Competitive Moat & 5-Year Roadmap

## 1. Beachhead Market Selection (Mind Map 4.1)
- **Suburban Corridor Sweet Spot (`8,000–15,000 pop/sq mi`):** Low airspace complexity and abundant standalone commercial rooftops paired with long car drive times and high `$8–$15` gig driver costs.
- **Vertical 1 — QSR Chains:** High daily volume, standardized `1.5–3.5 kg` hot meal packaging, flat rooftop access.
- **Vertical 2 — Grocery & Pharmacy:** High repeat frequency, urgent `2–5 kg` chilled/ambient baskets.
- **Vertical 3 — Medical Logistics:** STAT lab samples, biologics, and urgent prescriptions serving as a high-margin pricing anchor.

## 2. Go-to-Market Motion & Flywheel (Mind Map 4.2)
- **"Drone Port as a Service":** SkyNest installs the automated rooftop port for **$0 upfront** under a per-delivery fee + revenue-share agreement.
- **Channel Partnerships:** POS providers (**Toast, Square, Olo**), suburban retail **REITs** (blanket rooftop rights), and aviation insurers.
- **Compounding Expansion Loop:**
  `Merchant Density → Route Density → Lower Unit Cost ($1.50) → Lower Merchant Fee ($3–5) → More Merchants`
